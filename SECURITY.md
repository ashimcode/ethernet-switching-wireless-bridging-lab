# Lab Safety and Responsible Use

This repository documents an isolated educational network lab. All testing must remain within the GNS3 topology and systems owned or explicitly authorized for use.

- Do not connect the lab topology to production or third-party networks.
- Do not use real credentials or personal data in the topology.
- Verify VLAN, IP, and capture settings before starting tests.
- Keep raw packet captures outside the public repository unless they have been reviewed and sanitized.
- Remove hostnames, usernames, IP addresses, or other details that are not necessary for the learning objective.
- Preserve the original course files locally and do not redistribute them without permission.
